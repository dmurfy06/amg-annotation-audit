OCEAN (Microbiome 2024) — RULE APPLIED
==============================================================================
  Their rule: AMGs excluded if on contigs carrying "transposons, lipopolysaccharide
  islands (glycosyltransferase, ...), endonucleases, integrases, or plasmid stability".

                             permissive   conservative   verdict
  ------------------------ ------------ --------------   ------------------------
  glycosyltransferase            30,483              2   99.993% removed
  transposon (T flag)               762              0   removed entirely
  auxiliary score <= 3                  ['1', '2', '3']   respected

  VERDICT: the ocean catalogue APPLIES its own stated exclusions. 2 glycosyltransferase
  calls survive out of 30,483 — a 99.99% exclusion rate — and no transposon call does.
  This is a NEGATIVE result for H4, and it is what makes the positive one below credible.

WASTEWATER (ES&T 2023) — RULE UNDER-DETERMINED, not cleanly violated
==============================================================================
  Their rule: metabolic genes "directly involved in viral replication (e.g.,
  replication, repair, nucleotide transport, and metabolism) were not included".
  Their annotation: kofamscan against KEGG. So KEGG assigns the categories, not us.

  calls with a KEGG KO: 77 of 101
  KOs KEGG cannot categorise: 0

  KEGG 09104 Nucleotide metabolism      10 calls   13.0% [7.2-22.3]
  KEGG 09124 Replication and repair      0 calls    0.0% [0.0-4.8]
  --------------------------------------------------------------
  in an excluded category at all       10 calls   13.0% [7.2-22.3]
  ...EXCLUSIVELY so (a clean violation)    0 calls    0.0% [0.0-4.8]

  Every KO involved, with ALL the categories KEGG gives it:
        8  K01939  adenylosuccinate synthase [EC:6.3.4.4]
                      09104 Nucleotide metabolism  <- excluded by their rule
                      09105 Amino acid metabolism  (permitted)
        2  K00948  ribose-phosphate pyrophosphokinase [EC:2.7.6
                      09101 Carbohydrate metabolism  (permitted)
                      09104 Nucleotide metabolism  <- excluded by their rule

  >> THE HEADLINE DOES NOT SURVIVE. Both KOs are DUAL-CLASSIFIED: each also sits in a
     category the rule permits, so the authors could read purA as amino acid metabolism
     and prsA as carbohydrate metabolism — and they do present prsA as a carbon gene.
     ZERO calls are exclusively in an excluded category.

  >> What survives is better than a gotcha, and it is a finding about the rule itself:
     the stated criterion CANNOT BE APPLIED DETERMINISTICALLY. Using the authors' own
     annotation database, a gene can satisfy and violate their rule simultaneously,
     depending on which of KEGG's categories you happen to read. A rule that does not
     decide is not being enforced — it is being interpreted, case by case, invisibly.

WHAT CHUNK 4 ESTABLISHES
==============================================================================
  1. OCEAN states a mechanical exclusion and APPLIES it — 30,483 glycosyltransferase
     calls down to 2, every transposon call gone. Rules can be, and are, enforced.

  2. WASTEWATER states an exclusion that CANNOT BE APPLIED DETERMINISTICALLY. 13% of
     its KO-assigned calls carry a category its rule excludes, but ZERO carry one
     exclusively — every such gene also sits in a permitted category. The rule does
     not decide, so it is being interpreted case by case, invisibly, rather than
     enforced. That is a finding about the rule, not an accusation about the authors.

  3. SOIL states its rule in ordinary English, so it cannot be tested without our own
     judgement entering. Not tested here; it goes to the Chunk 5 adjudication.

  THE PRE-REGISTERED EXPECTATION FAILED, and that is reported rather than buried:
  H4 predicted the QUEUOSINE genes would violate the wastewater rule, because the
  authors themselves note those genes 'could also participate in tRNA biogenesis'.
  They do not trip the test. KEGG files queuosine biosynthesis under FOLATE
  BIOSYNTHESIS (09108, cofactors and vitamins), not nucleotide metabolism. The genes
  that do trip it are purA and prsA — and neither trips it cleanly.
