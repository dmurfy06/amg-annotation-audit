WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE WRITE-UP
======================================================================================
1. Strict exclusion in the ocean catalogue is 18.31% with `dcm` and 0.07% without it.
   The strict rule is, for practical purposes, a statement about one gene family.

2. The cross-environment claim survives only under the maximally-strict rule, where
   `folate` and `queuosine` - both UNRESOLVABLE, both genuinely contested - do the
   work. That is the defensible headline, and it should be stated as resting on
   unresolved families rather than on ruled-out ones.

3. Wastewater has zero strict exclusion. Its 37.66%% maximally-strict figure is
   entirely `folate` + `queuosine` on a denominator of 77 calls, CI [27.7-48.8].
   Report the interval every time; the point estimate alone overstates the precision.

4. Only 4 of 35 families were ruled out, and 3 of those 4 are protocol controls or
   near-zero in abundance. The adjudication's main finding is not that the record is
   full of miscounted families - it is that most families cannot be ruled out on
   current evidence, and that the disputed mass sits in three unresolvable ones.
