CHUNK 6 - ABUNDANCE-WEIGHTED vs CALL-WEIGHTED DISPUTED SHARE
========================================================================================
Call-weighted asks how much of the RECORD is disputed.
Abundance-weighted asks how much of the COMMUNITY that disputed record describes.

!! OCEAN CANNOT BE WEIGHTED. The curated ocean catalogue (Microbiome 2024) publishes
   annotations without a per-sample abundance table - 88,729 of 93,413 calls, and the
   catalogue carrying the entire strict-rule result. Nothing below extrapolates to it.

Abundance is published PER VIRUS in both catalogues that have it, so the naive sum
over calls double-counts gene-dense genomes. All three weightings are shown; the
per-call column is included only to expose the size of that bias.

========================================================================================
IS THE AGGREGATE RATIO A PROPERTY OF THE FAMILIES, OR OF THE ABUNDANCE SKEW?
========================================================================================
An aggregate ratio below 1.0 can mean disputed families sit on rarer viruses - or
merely that a few very abundant viruses happen to fall outside them. The direct
test is the median abundance of the viruses actually carrying each family.

Soil, one value per virus (mean across the 9 samples):
  carrying a disputed-family AMG   n=57   median 0.00010   mean 0.00025
  all other AMG viruses            n=482  median 0.00014   mean 0.00040
  ratio of medians 0.70x   ratio of means 0.63x

  mean/median ratio: disputed 2.6x, other 2.9x - both distributions are heavily
  right-skewed, which is what makes the aggregate share unstable.

  Per-family median abundance of carrier viruses, against the overall median:
    dcm                  n=2    median 0.00074   5.35x
    folate               n=39   median 0.00013   0.92x
    glycoside_hydrolase  n=4    median 0.00004   0.28x
    queuosine            n=33   median 0.00012   0.90x
    xtmA                 n=3    median 0.00004   0.26x
    xtmB                 n=1    median 0.00011   0.77x

========================================================================================
WHAT THIS ACTUALLY SHOWS
========================================================================================
1. The strict-rule 'depletion' is an artefact. Naive per-call summing gives 0.58x;
   counting each virus once gives 0.99-1.01x. There is no effect. Any figure
   computed by summing per-virus abundance across calls is wrong in this data.

2. Under the maximally-strict rule the abundance-weighted share IS lower than the
   call-weighted share, in both catalogues and under both correct weightings
   (soil 0.47-0.49x, wastewater 0.36-0.37x).

3. But that is NOT because folate- and queuosine-carrying viruses are rare. Their
   carrier viruses sit at 0.90x and 0.92x of the overall median - which is to say,
   typical. The aggregate gap is driven by the right skew of the abundance
   distribution: a small number of very abundant viruses carry no disputed AMG, and
   with 57 disputed viruses out of 539 that is not a stable property of the record.

   The defensible statement is therefore the narrow one: in these two small
   catalogues, abundance weighting LOWERS the disputed share, but the families
   driving the dispute are carried by viruses of ordinary abundance. Claiming that
   disputed genes sit on rare viruses would over-read the data.

4. Direction of effect, declared: this cuts AGAINST the project's hypothesis. The
   abundance-weighted numbers are smaller than the call-weighted ones, so the
   headline is weaker under the weighting the pre-registration committed to.
