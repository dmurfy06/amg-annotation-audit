CHUNK 7 - DO THE DISPUTED GENES CHANGE A PUBLISHED CLAIM?
========================================================================================
One claim per catalogue, taken from its own abstract, each recomputed under the
adjudication's four rules. Claims were fixed before any was recomputed.

========================================================================================
OCEAN (Microbiome 2024) - "~19% of ocean virus populations carry at least one AMG"
========================================================================================
A virus counts as AMG-carrying if it has at least one AMG call. Exclude a family and
any virus whose calls were ALL in excluded families stops qualifying. Both namespaces
are used, since dcm carries 5,797 KO and 5,381 Pfam calls in this catalogue.

virus contigs carrying >=1 AMG call, as published: 60471

rule                          still AMG       lost  claim becomes
------------------------------------------------------------------
As published (inclusive)          60471          0          19.0%
Strict                            55504       4967          17.4%
Maximally strict                  53885       6586          16.9%

Viruses whose ENTIRE AMG content is disputed (these are the ones that drop out):
    dcm                            4945
    queuosine                      702
    folate+queuosine               620
    folate                         255
    dcm+queuosine                  31
    glycoside_hydrolase            22

The claim moves from 19% to 16.9%. Note what carries that: 4945 viruses qualify as
AMG-carrying solely on the strength of `dcm` - a family whose verdict the protocol
fixed in advance as a worked example, and which Martin et al. name explicitly.

========================================================================================
THE PATTERN, AND IT IS THE RESULT
========================================================================================
The three claims are affected very differently, and the ordering is not arbitrary -
it tracks how specifically each claim depends on the disputed families.

  DESTROYED - wastewater. The claim names the queuosine genes as the most common
    vAMGs. Those genes are the disputed family. 29 of 101 vAMGs are removed and the
    subject of the sentence no longer exists; cysH inherits the top of the list.
    A claim ABOUT a disputed family cannot survive that family being disputed.

  MOVED, MODESTLY - ocean. "~19% of virus populations carry at least one AMG"
    becomes 17.4% (strict) or 16.9% (maximally strict) - a relative fall of about
    11%. Real, worth reporting, not fatal. 4,945 virus populations are AMG-carrying
    on the strength of `dcm` alone.

  UNTOUCHED - soil. The contamination gradient holds at 1.25x abundance and 2.54x
    richness under every rule. Disputed families are a roughly constant fraction
    across the gradient, so they cancel in the comparison.

The generalisation worth making: disputed families CANCEL in comparisons and
PERSIST in descriptions. A paper asking 'does AMG content differ between conditions?'
is largely safe. A paper asking 'what are these viruses doing?' is answering with
families the evidence cannot currently resolve - and the more specific the answer,
the more of it rests on them.

Direction of effect, declared: every recomputation here REDUCES the published
figure. None of the three claims is strengthened by the adjudication. Equally, none
of the three papers did anything the field would call wrong - they applied the
standard category. That is the point.
